The Banket logo (official PNG artwork) — sidebar top, login, PDF covers, emails.

```jsx
<Logo height={30} />
<Logo variant="mark" height={28} />
<Logo tone="light" height={28} />
```

- Never recolor the mark, add effects, or redraw it. The terracotta tile is `#E35336` (`--brand`).
- Clear space ≥ the height of the "b" tile's corner radius on all sides.
- `tone="light"` is a programmatic white-wordmark derivative of `logo-dark.png` for dark surfaces.
